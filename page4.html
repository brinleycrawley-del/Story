Gone.

"NOOOOOOOOO!" Your body lets loose a sound hidden deep in your core...one buried for far too long...

You fall to your knees, tears streaming down your face.

The tears roll down your cheeks and pummel the ground as your racking chest heaves with grief.

You cry and cry...

You feel more and more empty with each breath...

You can't remember how long you were kneeling on the ground that day, it felt like an eternity.

At some point, your eyes are puffy and red and the tears stop flowing.

The sobs turn to sniffling.

You turn over your fist and find the note

Hold on...
