Tomorrow...

You're walking home after a long day, the longest day of your life. You feel exhausted. You feel the weight of the bags under your eyes and the world on your shoulders.

"Just one more day..." you say to yourself. Tomorrow is the last day of work, the last day of school, the last day of your existence. After tomorrow, you can finally rest...

While you're walking along the cracked sidewalk lost in thought, hearing the crunch of autumn leaves under your soles and the cool evening breeze through your hair, something cuts through your mind's eye and steals your attention away from your evening plans...

A torn piece of paper...
