"I miss you too...but you can't go on like this..."

"Mom!" you scream, clambering to your feet and running after the apparition.

"Please don't leave me! Please not again!" You feel the flood of memories rushing back into view, the memories you tried so hard to forget.

You remember your first day of school and how she wrote you a note on your lunch napkin saying, "Have a good first ever day of school! <3 You!"...

You remember how you would help her cook dinner and how she said you were such a good stirrer...

You remember how you yelled at her because you thought she would never understand...

You remember seeing her get very sick...

You remember all the hospital visits...

You remember all your regrets...

All that you wish you could take back...

You reach out your hand to try and hug her one last time...
