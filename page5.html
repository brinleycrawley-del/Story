Dawn Breaks Through the Sky

You look back at the note and think back to the eyes that met you on your path...

You think back to the ringing sound and it comes to your attention that the cacophony of everyday life finally ceased.

There's no more noise...Just stillness...Just peace...

You let out a long, heavy sigh...and with weary eyes and an even wearier heart,

You take a deep breath and muster up all the strength left in you to climb to your feet.

"Okay mom... I'll hold on a little longer."

Then you walk home with a new plan for tomorrow...
