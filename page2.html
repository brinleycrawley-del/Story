Hold on...

Reading the two words brings tears to your eyes.

"Hold on...? Don't you know how hard I've tried?" you mutter under trembling breath.

You hold onto this piece of torn parchment like a lifeline, and with hands shaking, you crumble to the earth sobbing.

Through the heaving of your breath and the tears streaming down your face, you hear a faint ringing in the distance

You lift your head to the sound and lock eyes with another too-familiar pair.

She's smiling back at you...
